# Example prompts and prompt-history.md

This file contains sample prompts used to guide the LLM for email generation and development.

## Email generation prompt (used in src/ai/email_generator.py)

Write a short, friendly cold outreach email to {business_name}. The business website is {website}. The Google rating is {rating}. Try to identify likely pain points for a small local business and propose a clear single next step. Keep subject line under 8 words and body under 220 words.

## Development prompts (examples):
- "Scaffold a Streamlit app that triggers a Python orchestration script and streams logs to the UI."
- "Write a SerpAPI wrapper to query Google Maps 'local results' and return name, website, phone, rating."
